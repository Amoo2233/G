About the studio

Small team. Serious work.

Meridian was founded in 2017 on a simple bet: a small team of genuinely senior people will out-ship an agency ten times its size. Nine years and 120 launches later, the bet keeps paying.

Our story

Built by people who got tired of the handoff

We started Meridian after years inside big agencies watching great strategy die in the handoff to design, and great design die in the handoff to engineering. So we removed the handoffs. Every Meridian project is run by one team that owns the idea from the first workshop to the last deploy.

No account managers. No junior layer learning on your budget. Just fourteen practitioners who have each shipped for a decade or more — and still argue about kerning.

What we believe

Four rules we won't break

Ship the truth

Prototypes over promises. If we can't show it working, we don't claim it's done.

Deadlines are design

A launch date is a constraint like any other. We scope to hit it, not to explain missing it.

Disagree in the room

We tell clients when we think they're wrong — before the invoice, not after the launch.

Numbers or it didn't happen

Every project defines its success metric up front. We report against it, win or lose.

The road so far

Nine years in five moments

2017

Three desks in Lisbon

Two designers and an engineer quit their agency jobs, sign one brave client, and name the studio after the line every map depends on.

2019

First award, first hire wave

An e-commerce rebuild wins our first international design award. The team grows to eight — every hire with a decade of shipped work behind them.

2021

Remote-first, for real

We close the office and rebuild the studio around deep-work weeks and quarterly on-sites. Client satisfaction goes up, not down.

2024

100th launch

A hundred products, sites and brands shipped. We celebrate by publishing our full process playbook — clients deserve to see how the sausage is made.

2026

Fourteen people, four continents

Still small on purpose. Still senior only. Still arguing about kerning.
