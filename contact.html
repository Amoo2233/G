Good to know

What happens after you hit send

What does the first call look like?+
Forty-five minutes with a partner — not a salesperson, because we don't have any. We'll ask about the problem, the timeline and how you'll measure success. You'll leave with our honest read, even if the honest read is "you don't need us."
How soon will we get a proposal?+
Within a week of the first call, usually sooner. It includes a fixed price, a named team, a week-by-week plan and the success metric we're signing up for.
Do you sign NDAs?+
Happily. Send yours or use ours — either way it's signed before you share anything sensitive.
We're not sure of our budget. Should we still write?+
Yes. Pick "Not sure yet" in the form and describe the problem. We'll tell you what similar projects have cost so you can decide with real numbers instead of guesses.
