Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

We collect the minimum data needed to reply to you and to understand how this site is used. We don't sell data, we don't run ad trackers, and we delete what we no longer need.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, company and message — used only to reply to your enquiry.
  • Newsletter: your email address — used only to send the monthly note you asked for. Unsubscribe anytime with one click.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme choice is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of personal data, ever.
  • No third-party advertising trackers or fingerprinting.
  • No adding you to marketing lists you didn't ask to join.

Retention

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as we're in conversation, then archived. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion. Email hello@meridian.studio and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Questions

Anything unclear? Write to hello@meridian.studio — a person, not a bot, will answer.
