What's inside

Small file. Big energy.

Everything on this page is hand-rolled: the starfield, the aurora, the 3D-tilting cards, the scroll reveals. View source — it's all right there.

✦

Living starfield

A canvas of drifting particles that link into constellations and bend toward your cursor like gravity.

◎

Aurora engine

Three blurred radial gradients on screen blend-mode, drifting on offset loops — the whole sky in ~10 lines of CSS.

⌁

3D tilt cards

These cards track your pointer and rotate in 3D space with layered depth. Go on — hover me.

✧

Scroll choreography

IntersectionObserver reveals each section as it enters the viewport. No scroll libraries, no jank.
